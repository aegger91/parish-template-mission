## Images

No photographs or third-party images are included. Every image on the site is a drawn placeholder made for this template:

- The eight-pointed cross (`src/components/brand/OrthodoxCross.tsx`), used everywhere a cross appears, including the favicon and sample logo.
- Line pictograms (`src/components/brand/Pictogram.tsx`): church, chalice, censer, candle, prayer rope, Gospel book, icon, bells, lampada, commemoration slip, font, analogion, chrism, crowns, unction, holy water, kanun.
- Placeholder art (`src/components/brand/PlaceholderArt.tsx`), marked "Sample image" on the page.
- `public/favicon.svg` and `public/brand/sample-logo-square.svg`.

Two general icons (utensils, mail) and the interface icons come from Lucide (ISC license).

If a parish adds public-domain images, for example from Wikimedia Commons, list each one here with its title, author, license and URL.
